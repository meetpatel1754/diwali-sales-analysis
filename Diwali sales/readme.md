# Diwali Sales Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes Diwali sales data to identify customer purchasing
patterns, high-performing product categories, and geographic sales trends.

The goal is to generate actionable business insights that can help
businesses improve marketing strategies and sales performance.

## 2. Business Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Identify high-revenue customer segments.
- Find the highest-performing states and zones.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Analyze sales across gender, age groups, and occupations.
- Provide data-driven business recommendations.

## 5. Data Cleaning

The dataset was checked for:

- Missing values
- Duplicate records
- Unnecessary columns
- Data types

The columns containing entirely missing values were removed.
Rows with missing Amount values were excluded, and duplicate records
were removed.

## 6. SQL Analysis

SQL was used to analyze:

- Total revenue and total orders
- Unique customers
- Revenue by gender and age group
- Sales by state and zone
- Product category performance
- Customer segmentation
- Revenue contribution by customer segment

## 7. Power BI Dashboard

The interactive dashboard includes:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Unique Customers
- Average Order Value
- Revenue by Gender
- Revenue by Age Group
- Revenue by Product Category
- Top States by Revenue
- Revenue by Occupation

Interactive slicers allow users to explore the data by gender,
age group, product category, and state.

## 8. Key Business Insights

- Female customers contributed a higher share of total revenue
  than male customers.
- The 26–35 age group generated the highest revenue among age groups.
- Uttar Pradesh was the highest-revenue state in the dashboard.
- Food was the highest-revenue product category in the dashboard.
- IT Sector customers generated the highest revenue among occupations.
- Customer segmentation showed that the High Value segment
  contributed approximately 94.41% of revenue in the SQL analysis.

Note: These findings are based on the current analysis and should be
verified against the final cleaned dataset and dashboard figures.

## 9. Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing campaigns on high-performing customer segments.
- Prioritize inventory planning for top-performing product categories.
- Review sales performance across states to improve regional marketing.
- Use customer purchasing patterns to design targeted campaigns.
- Investigate lower-performing categories for potential improvement.
- Monitor sales KPIs regularly through the dashboard.
